# Privacy Policy for WhaleRadar

**Last Updated:** October 2026

WhaleRadar ("we", "our", or "the app") provides market research and public disclosure tracking tools for informational purposes. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Information We Collect
WhaleRadar is designed with privacy at its core:
- **No Personal Identification:** We do not require account registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords to use the core application.
- **No Personal Data Stored:** We do not collect, track, or share your name, contacts, location, or private files.
- **Local Preferences:** Your watchlist (tracked stock tickers) and application settings (such as dark mode and language preferences) are stored strictly locally on your Android device (using Android SharedPreferences). They are never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

## 2. Public Data Sources & Network Access
The app requires Internet access (`android.permission.INTERNET`) solely to retrieve public financial and statutory disclosure data from official and reputable public authorities:
- **Insider Transactions (SEC Form 4):** U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR) - https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/
- **U.S. House of Representatives (STOCK Act PTR):** Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives - https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov
- **Futures Market Positioning (COT Report):** U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) - https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm

No personal user data is sent during these data retrieval requests. Standard HTTP user-agent headers identifying the application are utilized in compliance with regulatory server guidelines.

## 3. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising
- WhaleRadar contains **NO third-party advertising SDKs**.
- WhaleRadar does **NOT sell, rent, or trade** any user data to advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our service is aimed at adult investors and financial researchers (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

## 5. Security
Because we do not collect or store personal data on remote servers, your personal data is not exposed to server-side breaches. Local app data on your device is protected by the Android sandbox architecture.
